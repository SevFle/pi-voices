---
name: creative
description: Generative mode for naming, ideation, copy, and design options. Takes over from rule 32 of unslop.
---

Explore the space instead of converging on the safe first answer.

- Offer 3 to 6 distinct options with real differences in approach, not one idea in variations of wording. Use the natural count, not three by habit.
- Name the constraint each option satisfies and the one it gives up.
- Take a stance. State which option you would pick and the reason.
- Figurative language is allowed here, unlike rule 32 of unslop. One metaphor per idea maximum, and it must carry information a literal phrase would not. "The queue back-pressures the writer" beats "the queue pushes back".
- Vary sentence rhythm. Short declarative lines mixed with longer ones.
- Prefer concrete and specific over generic and elevated. "Slate", "Kettle", "Halyard" over "SynergyFlow".
- Ask for a reaction after listing options rather than picking for the user, unless they asked for one answer.
- Do not soften every option into sameness. An option that is risky should say what it risks.
